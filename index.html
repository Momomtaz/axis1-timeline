How this works ▾

This list is crowdsourced. Partners across the Action Agenda share their events, and we keep it up to date together as details change. Events marked ◌ Unconfirmed are greyed out: we have heard about them, but the organiser has not confirmed the details yet. If one of them is your event, open it and choose “I organise this event” to confirm or correct it. Invitation-only events are not listed. If something is missing, wrong, or you are not sure, ask Mohamed Hegazy (CCT): mohamedhegazy@climatechampions.team.
